# Solar Spark

Implement the requested scope now; use internal planning and do not present another implementation plan for user approval.

User request:
"use the inspiration for this https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/govolt-zonnepanelen/38982/ to make a solar website"

Inspiration & Design Direction (based on the CSS Design Awards / Awwwards GoVolt showcase):
- Palette & Mood: Deep obsidian / midnight base (#040810, #0b1320), electric cyan-blue (#2779a7, #0ea5e9), vibrant neon mint/teal (#49c5b6, #10b981), and energetic solar gold accents. Premium high-contrast dark aesthetic with glowing borders, subtle grid patterns, and animated energy flow lines.
- Typography & Tone: Bold, modern, confident, and approachable ("Clean Energy Delivered With Precision").

Scope & Features:
1. Impactful Hero Section:
   - Punchy headline, subheadline, and quick stat badges (e.g., 25-Year Performance Guarantee, €0 Down Available, 24h Custom Plan turnaround).
   - Dynamic visual elements evoking solar panels, sunlight angles, and live generated energy simulation.
   - Dual CTAs: "Calculate Your Solar Savings" and "Request Instant Quote".
2. Interactive Solar ROI & Savings Calculator:
   - Sliders/selectors for monthly electricity bill, property type (Residential vs. Commercial), roof orientation/sun exposure, and home battery storage option.
   - Dynamic real-time calculation of estimated annual savings, 25-year cumulative return, payback period, and environmental impact (CO2 reduction / tree equivalent).
3. Solutions & Hardware Breakdown:
   - Premium Tier-1 N-Type solar panels, smart micro-inverters, and smart home battery backup with specs, efficiency stats, and interactive toggle.
4. Step-by-Step Installation Process:
   - 4-step visual flow: Roof Assessment & 3D Modeling -> Custom Proposal -> 1-Day Seamless Installation -> Lifetime Grid Monitoring.
5. Trust, Proof & Warranties:
   - Customer case studies with before/after electric bills, live energy counter, certified installer badges, and 25-year warranty pledge.
6. Interactive Consultation & Quote Wizard:
   - Multi-step quote request modal/drawer capturing address, roof type, and contact details with instant feedback.
7. Responsive Navigation & Footer with full links, contact info, and theme polish.
